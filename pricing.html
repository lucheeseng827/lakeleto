Lakeleto Cloud (preview)

A hosted team plane for Lakeleto: shared workspaces and run history, a governed catalog over your own buckets, roles, table and column grants, and a tamper-evident audit log.

Not available yet. These are proposed prices, shown so you can tell us if they are wrong. Pricing is subject to change.

The Lakeleto tool stays free. The binary, the web grid, SQL and MCP are Apache-2.0, need no account and upload nothing. None of it depends on Cloud.

Proposed tiers.

The plan is to charge for the people who build and let viewers ride free on paid plans. Reaching a limit would pause new runs, never add a charge.

Free

$0forever, no card

  • Up to 3 members
  • 10 compute-hours a month
  • Catalog, grants, audit log and console included
  • Never expires

Pro

$19per member a month, or $15 billed yearly

  • 1 to 10 members, viewers free
  • 25 compute-hours a month
  • Everything in Free

Team

$39per member a month, or $32 billed yearly; 3-member minimum

  • Unlimited members, viewers free
  • 150 compute-hours a month
  • Priority support
  • A 14-day trial with no card is planned

Enterprise

Contactdedicated plane or self-hosted, annual

  • Negotiated limits
  • SSO, SLA and security review
  • Invoice billing

Proposed November 2026, in US dollars, before tax. Everything in these four blocks is a proposal: member counts, support, SSO and the SLA are not in place during the preview, and only the compute and run limits exist in the software today. Limits are measured over a trailing window, so capacity returns as older use ages out, with no reset day to wait for.

Questions.

Can I sign up?

Not yet. Lakeleto Cloud is in preview and no hosted plan is on sale. If these prices would or would not work for your team, tell us on GitHub.

Do I pay to scroll a table?

No. Browsing a table in the web grid, its schema and its stats is not billed per call. The only metered thing is compute time, and each plan includes a monthly amount.

What happens when I reach a limit?

New runs pause until the trailing window frees capacity, and you can upgrade. Nothing is billed for going over, and nothing is deleted.

Does Cloud change the free tool?

No. The tool is Apache-2.0, works offline, needs no account and stays that way. Cloud is a separate, optional product.

Discounts?

Under consideration, none decided: about 20% off for paying yearly, early-customer pricing, and free Pro for open-source maintainers, educators and non-profits.
